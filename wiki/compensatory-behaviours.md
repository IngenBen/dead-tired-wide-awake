A cluster of behaviours the author used to keep functioning despite chronic poor sleep: caffeine, sugar, daytime napping (15-minute semi-lucid rests on weekends and commutes), spending 9+ hours in bed, and sheer self-talk-driven willpower. These provided temporary relief but never resolved the underlying problem — and in some cases (extended time in bed) may have worsened sleep quality.
