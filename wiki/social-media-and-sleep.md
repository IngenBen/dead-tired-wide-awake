The blue light narrative is outdated — more recent evidence shows stimulating content delays sleep onset more than light exposure alone. Blue light blockers show modest, inconsistent benefit; content type shows larger effects.

Three-step approach:
1. **Non-negotiable first:** Phone face-down during any night waking. Do not check. Even a glance at a notification is enough to trigger the cortisol-dopamine cycle and end the night.
2. **Awareness before reduction:** Notice which content spikes emotion. Stop clicking it — not from discipline, but from understanding what it costs.
3. **Redirect the algorithm:** Click deliberately toward genuinely engaging, non-arousing content. The feed changes within days. Then reduce usage in the run-up to bed.
