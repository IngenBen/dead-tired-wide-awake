Cortisol is not the enemy — it's a rhythm problem. The design is: rise in the morning, spike and return in response to demand, fall away by evening. Modern life systematically prevents the "fall away" phase: notifications, stimulating content, late meals, blood sugar crashes, and the physiological stress of sleep deprivation itself all keep cortisol elevated past the point where the Sleep Executives can advance.

The cortisol-sleep feedback loop is central to the book: poor sleep → cortisol elevated → next night harder → poorer sleep.
