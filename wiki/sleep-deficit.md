A state in which accumulated lost sleep degrades daytime functioning. The author describes falling asleep the moment his head hit the pillow not as a good sign but as evidence of how deep the deficit ran — the body simply crashing. Despite spending nine or more hours in bed, actual sleep was only four or five broken hours.
