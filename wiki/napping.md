The key insight: the meditation nap solves both problems with traditional napping — (1) it provides real rest regardless of whether sleep occurs (the nervous system is trained and somewhat recovered), and (2) it cannot overshoot because the 15-minute alarm contains it. If sleep comes, it's a short nap that won't meaningfully deplete adenosine. If sleep doesn't come, it's a bonus calming system session with no adenosine cost.

The 8-hours-before-bed rule for traditional napping does not apply to the 15-min meditation nap, because the sleep duration is too short to meaningfully shift adenosine levels.
