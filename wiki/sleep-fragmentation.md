Waking multiple times per night, with difficulty returning to sleep. In ch01 this is a defining feature of the author's poor sleep — every night, not occasionally. Combined with low sleep efficiency (time in bed vs. actual sleep), fragmentation meant he woke only slightly less tired than when he went to bed.

Ch09 provides the mechanistic explanation for the 2–3am wake specifically: the circadian turning point where melatonin production tails off and adenosine has been largely spent, leaving the elevated cortisol of someone who sleeps badly to fill the gap.
