Ch01 is a first-person memoir of living with chronic poor sleep for over 20 years. Key beats: the paradox of falling asleep instantly (a deficit signal, not a good sign); the unpredictability of nights; the daily fog and afternoon crashes; the compensatory scaffolding of caffeine, sugar, and willpower; the temptation to accept this as genetic fate; and the stubborn refusal to give up. The emotional low point is reading to his children at night — physically present but barely conscious, unable to take in the words.
