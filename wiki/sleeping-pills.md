The book does not oppose sleeping pills and explicitly recommends a doctor conversation for short-term use. The critique is structural: they address the symptom (inability to sleep) by forcing the workers to clock off regardless of what the executives are doing — but the executives keep sending wake signals, which is why efficacy tends to degrade.
