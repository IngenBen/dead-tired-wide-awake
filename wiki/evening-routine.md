The evening routine exists to stop interfering with a process the body is already running. By the time dinner is done, cortisol is naturally declining and melatonin is rising. The routine's job is to avoid sending counter-signals (bright light, activating content, unresolved conversation) that tell the Wake Executives the day isn't over.

Practical structure: lights down post-dinner (desk lamps, not overhead). Undemanding TV. Clean stop when TV goes off — teeth, bed. No social media, no intense conversation, nothing that spikes dopamine or cortisol. Bed for sleep and intimacy only.

Bedtime should be ~8 hours before wake time. Science recommends within 30 min consistency; the author treats this as a guide, noting going earlier (if genuinely sleepy) costs little, going later costs more.
