The core framework of the book. From dozens of hormones involved in sleep, the author identified four as both the most influential and the most actionable. Two support sleep (adenosine builds pressure, melatonin signals timing); two support waking (cortisol drives alertness, dopamine drives motivation). Good sleep requires all four to follow their correct daily arc — rising and falling at the right times to arrive at the right state by bedtime. The CEO can't operate them directly; each does its best to align with the company culture, and the program's job is to get them working together.

Note: adenosine is technically a neurotransmitter, not a hormone; the book acknowledges this and uses "hormone" loosely for all four.
