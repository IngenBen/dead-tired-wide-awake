The core framework of the book. From dozens of hormones involved in sleep, the author picked four as both the most influential and the most actionable: a little less accurate than the full picture, much more useful. Two support sleep (adenosine builds pressure, melatonin signals timing) and two support waking (cortisol drives alertness, dopamine drives motivation). Good sleep needs all four to clock on and off at the right times, so they arrive at the right state by bedtime: sleep side high, wake side low. The CEO can't operate them directly; habits send them signals, and the same signals every day teach them their shifts.

Note: adenosine is technically a neuromodulator and dopamine mainly a neurotransmitter; the book acknowledges this (ch04 note 1) and uses "hormone" for all four.
