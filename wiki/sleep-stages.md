The book treats sleep quality as more than total hours — what happens during those hours matters. Deep sleep is where the body clears adenosine, repairs tissue, and regulates hormones. REM handles cognitive and emotional maintenance. Disruption of either — through alcohol, late eating, fragmented arousal — produces the felt experience of unrestorative sleep even when hours in bed were adequate.

Trackers measure sleep staging, but with limited nightly accuracy (~20–30 min error); weekly trends are reliable.
