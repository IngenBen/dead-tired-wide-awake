The author explicitly frames this as removing friction rather than optimising for performance. Three factors:

**Light:** Even low-level light produces mild alertness. Blackout (recessed shutters or blinds with no gaps) is the target; tape over LED indicators. Goal: barely see a hand silhouette in the dark.

**Temperature:** Core body temperature drops ~1–2°C at sleep onset — the bedroom must allow this. Thin cotton layers allow easy adjustment. A fan for hot nights. Not about achieving a precise number; about letting the body regulate itself.

**Noise:** Sudden sounds fragment sleep without waking. Eliminate source first; earplugs as fallback. (Dog collar anecdote: citronella collar stopped barking that was breaking the sleep window.)

**TV:** Two harms — stimulating content at wind-down time, and training bed-wakefulness association. Remove it.
