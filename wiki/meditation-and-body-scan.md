The reframe in ch20 is structural: meditation is not an aspirin for a racing mind — it is training the strength the calming system needs to work when called on. This explains why the results appear weeks in, not session by session.

**Method:**
- Lie flat, same position each time, earbuds with consistent audio (528 Hz or similar — unique audio builds conditioned response)
- Body scan: move attention through the body one area at a time, inhale into each area, exhale and feel it soften; pause after exhale
- Breathing: nasal only (~5s in, 5s out, 5s pause); longer exhale:inhale ratio = more time decelerating heart rate
- Duration: 10–15 min; daily consistency > occasional long sessions

**Three triggers:** bedtime, morning (training, not calming), middle of night (place before getting up from bed).

Note: ch20 acknowledges this is a simplification of neural signal architecture (footnote).
