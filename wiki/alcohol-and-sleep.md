Three distinct disruption mechanisms:

1. **REM suppression and deep sleep fragmentation:** Reduces restorative sleep — ~15 min per 1–2 drinks, up to 40+ min for 3–4 drinks. Deep sleep fragmentation also prevents adenosine clearance, worsening next-morning grogginess.

2. **Metabolic rebound:** After heavier nights, the body shifts from suppressed to aroused mid-sleep (~4am), producing a wide-awake, heart-racing episode at the worst recovery point of the night.

3. **REM rebound:** REM suppressed early triggers compensatory intense/vivid dreams later, disrupting sleep.

The book acknowledges the science ("zero alcohol is the recommended position") but explicitly offers a pragmatic compromise: dry working week + weekend drinking kept early, with food, and minimal volume.
