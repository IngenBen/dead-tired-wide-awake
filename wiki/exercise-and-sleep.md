The core paradox (ch02): full-day intense exercise reliably produced worse sleep, not better. Mechanism: cortisol spikes during effort; in a sleep-deprived person with a weak calming system, it doesn't clear before the sleep window. Body temperature elevation adds a second mechanism — the body needs to cool to initiate sleep and intense exercise can delay that by 30 min to 2 hours.

WHOOP insight: sleep-deprived people need less exercise to hit their recovery-appropriate strain targets than they think. A morning walk plus light gym session can be sufficient.

**Rules:**
- Intensity: morning preferred; afternoon acceptable (finish 4pm+ assuming 10pm bedtime; author uses 6-hour margin)
- Evening: low-intensity only — walk, gentle swim, yoga/somatic
- Unavoidable late intense session: cold shower, long stretch, body scan as comedown

Yoga is explicitly favoured over other evening options because it also trains the calming system via the same bottom-up vagus nerve channel as meditation.
