Cold turkey fails because sleep deprivation measurably reduces capacity for self-control — asking the most depleted version of yourself to make the hardest possible changes. The incremental method works by never requiring more willpower than the depleted state can provide. Each small move, normalised before the next, compounds over weeks into large change that sticks.

Applied to caffeine: move the last coffee 5–10 minutes earlier, pause until normal, repeat.
Applied to alcohol: start with one dry weeknight, expand.
Applied to sugar: reduce one unit at a time (sugars in coffee, evening treats).

The method is explicitly framed as the same principle across all habits — not a caffeine-specific trick but a general change architecture.
