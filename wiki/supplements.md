All three supplements act on cortisol regulation rather than sedation:

**Phosphatidylserine (PS):** Soy- or sunflower-derived lipid that blunts evening cortisol response. 300–500mg, 30–60 min before bed.

**Ashwagandha KSM-66:** Root extract standardised to minimum concentration. Reduces cortisol stress response. 300–600mg, late afternoon or evening. Must specify KSM-66 — generic ashwagandha is less effective.

**Magnesium L-threonate:** Best-absorbed form for sleep and cortisol regulation. 150–300mg, evening. Citrate/glycinate are acceptable alternatives.

Protocol: introduce one at a time over days (not all at once) to isolate any adverse reactions. Author takes all three after dinner for adherence simplicity.

The CBN anecdote in ch03 is thematically connected — it represents the "silver bullet" approach the supplements chapter explicitly moves away from, positioning these three as part of a system rather than a fix.
