The gap between time spent in bed and time actually asleep. The author's experience (9+ hours in bed, 4–5 hours of broken sleep) illustrates low sleep efficiency. Spending excessive time in bed is a common compensatory response to poor sleep that can entrench insomnia patterns rather than resolving them.
