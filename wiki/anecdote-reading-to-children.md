The author describes sitting with his children for bedtime reading — a moment that should be simple and good — but being so cognitively fogged he read aloud without taking in the words, focused only on staying awake to the end of the chapter. This anecdote serves as the emotional anchor for the whole book: the cost of poor sleep is not abstract; it is the ordinary moments it steals.
