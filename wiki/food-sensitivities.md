Positioned in the book as a "final piece" for people who have done the full program and still notice something is off. The author found dairy sensitivity as the cause of persistent mild nasal congestion, which fragments sleep through impaired breathing.

⚠️ Status flagged unsure — the book explicitly notes "the science is contested" and that blood test results "should be treated as a prompt for further investigation, not a diagnosis." Should not be cited as established science.

notes: Endnote 'Are IgG food-sensitivity tests reliable?' rewritten 2026-10-28 (author decision) to match the chapter's stance: leads with wide use and two small supportive trials (IgG-guided elimination in IBS, Atkinson 2004; in migraine, Alpay 2010), then the allergy-body position (ASCIA, EAACI, AAAAI) and the advice to confirm by elimination and reintroduction. Status stays unsure.
