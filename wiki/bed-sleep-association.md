Stimulus control is one of the central mechanisms of CBT-i (Cognitive Behavioural Therapy for Insomnia). The book arrives at the same principle through its hormonal framework: every episode of lying awake anxious or stimulated in bed teaches the brain that bed is a context for wakefulness, eventually making the bed itself a trigger for arousal.

The can't-sleep routine (get up, dim room, low engagement) serves two functions: immediate cortisol/dopamine management, and long-term association rewriting. "The lesson is being rewritten, slowly, in the language the brain actually speaks."
