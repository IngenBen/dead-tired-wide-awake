The tracker is positioned as the first tool to acquire (Phase 1), before any habit changes, because it provides a baseline and then signals whether changes are working. Without it, changes are made in the dark.

Three-layer hierarchy of what to watch:
1. **Duration, consistency, efficiency** — start here; fix these first
2. **Sleep staging (deep/REM)** — quality; watch as weekly trends not nightly numbers
3. **HRV and recovery** — refinement; later

Tracker accuracy: total sleep time is reliable (adjust for still-waking periods); nightly staging has ~20–30 min error; weekly staging trends narrow to ~10 min. Oura Ring has most published research; WHOOP has strong research reputation and less bulk.

HRV (heart rate variability) reflects how well the nervous system moves between activated and calm states — higher variability = better recovery.
