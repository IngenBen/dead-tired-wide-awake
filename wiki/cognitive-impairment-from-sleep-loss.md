In ch01, cognitive impairment from sleep loss is shown entirely through experience: reading aloud to children without taking in the words; spending days feeling on the verge of falling asleep; filtering irritability through sheer effort. No mechanism is described yet — this is the felt reality the science chapters will later explain.
