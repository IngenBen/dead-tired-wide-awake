canonical: The primary physical pathway of the parasympathetic (Calming) nervous system. Extends from brainstem through heart, lungs, and gut. Slow, extended exhalation directly stimulates it — the mechanism by which breathing exercises and meditation train the calming system.
---

Breathing is the only autonomic function that can be taken off autopilot, making it the only direct access point to the vagus nerve and the calming system. The slow exhale works mechanically: when the diaphragm and lungs decelerate, the vagus nerve sends a safety signal that suppresses the cortisol stress response. This is what ch20 means by "bottom-up" regulation — the body sends information up to the brain rather than the brain driving activation downward ("top-down").

A trained vagus nerve means the calming system can hold its ground against the activating system, particularly at night.
