## Findings (close summary, not copied text)

- Meta-analysis of 94 independent tests of implementation intentions ('if situation X arises, I will do Y').
- If-then plans improved goal attainment over goal intentions alone, d = 0.65 (medium to large).
- (As reported in Sheeran & Webb 2016.) If-then plans help people remember to act, seize opportunities, resist distractions and temptations, and disengage from futile goals.

### If-Then Plans Strongly Boost Follow-Through

In a 2006 analysis, psychologists Peter Gollwitzer and Paschal Sheeran pooled 94 tests of a simple planning technique: deciding in advance exactly when, where and how you will act, in an 'if this happens, then I will do that' form.

People who made if-then plans were considerably more likely to reach their goals than people who only set the goal. The plans seem to work by making the right moment easier to spot and the response more automatic.

**The main takeaway:** Deciding in advance exactly when and how you will act makes you much more likely to follow through.
