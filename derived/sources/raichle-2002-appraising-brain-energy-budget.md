## Findings (close summary, not copied text)

- The adult brain is about 2% of body weight but accounts for about 20% of the body's oxygen use, and so its calories.
- This high metabolic rate is remarkably constant despite widely varying mental and motor activity.
- Most of the brain's energy goes on signalling between neurons (action potentials and restoring ion balance after synaptic activity), not on maintaining resting cells.

### Brain Uses a Fifth of Body Energy, Whatever You Think

In a 2002 commentary at Washington University School of Medicine in the USA, neuroscientists Marcus Raichle and Debra Gusnard set out what is known about how much energy the brain uses and where it goes.

They report that the brain makes up about 2% of body weight but uses about a fifth of the body's oxygen and calories. That rate barely changes whether a person is resting or busy with a demanding mental or physical task. Most of the energy goes on the constant signalling between brain cells.

**The main takeaway:** The brain is very expensive to run, but it costs about the same whether you are thinking hard or not.
