## Findings (close summary, not copied text)

- 21 studies, 24 effect sizes, 15,907 participants.
- Mental contrasting with implementation intentions (MCII) improved goal attainment, g = 0.34 (small to medium).
- Stronger when delivered in person by an experimenter (g = 0.47) than through written materials (g = 0.28).
- Some publication bias, so the true effect may be smaller; few studies for testing moderators.

### Picturing Obstacles and Planning Helps Reach Goals

In a 2021 analysis at Northeast Normal University in China, researchers pooled 21 studies involving nearly 16,000 people that tested a technique called mental contrasting with implementation intentions.

People first picture the outcome they want, then the main obstacle inside themselves that could block it, then make an if-then plan for that obstacle. Across studies, people who used it were somewhat more likely to reach their goals, especially when someone walked them through it in person.

**The main takeaway:** Pairing a goal with a clear look at the obstacle and a plan for it gives a modest but real boost to follow-through.
