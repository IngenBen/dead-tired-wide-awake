### Brain May Weigh Effort Against Payoff Before Trying

In a 2013 theory paper at Princeton University in the USA, neuroscientists Amitai Shenhav, Matthew Botvinick and Jonathan Cohen proposed a single explanation for what a brain region called the dorsal anterior cingulate cortex does.

They argue it works out whether deliberate, effortful control is worth applying, weighing the expected payoff against how much control is needed and how costly the effort feels. They then show how this idea fits a wide range of earlier findings.

**The main takeaway:** A leading theory holds that the brain decides whether effort is worth it before committing to it.
