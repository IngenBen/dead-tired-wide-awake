# Program Naming — Sleep Builders

**Working name:** Sleep Builders  
**Decided:** 2026-06-04  
**Status:** Working title — not finalised

---

## What the name does

**Sleep Builders** works on three levels simultaneously:

- **Course:** "I did Sleep Builders"
- **Community:** "the Sleep Builders group"
- **Identity:** "I'm a Sleep Builder"

The identity level is the most important. The "-ers" suffix creates tribal belonging — you don't just complete the program, you become a Sleep Builder. This aligns with the book's habit philosophy: identity-based habits stick because you're acting in accordance with who you are, not just what you want.

---

## Why "build" is the right verb

"Build" is the connective tissue of the book's argument:

- You build habits (the method)
- You build your daily rhythm (what you do)
- You build the calm/quiet (the nervous system outcome)
- You build your sleep fitness (the result)

Every answer to "how?" uses the same verb. This makes the program name internally consistent with the book's language in a way that "Own," "Fix," or "Find" would not be.

---

## What was considered and rejected

The naming process explored several directions before landing here:

**Corporate/executive metaphor** (Sleep Incorporated, Run the 24, Own the Calm, Align the 4) — strong connection to the book's internal framework but too abstract without the book.

**Day→night arc** (Rise to Rest, Day to Deep, Work to Rest, Night and Day) — captured the 24-hour insight well but didn't connect back to the book's world.

**Quiet as destination** (Build the Quiet, Racing to Quiet, Wired to Quiet, Quietly Built, Quiet the Noise) — strongest conceptual direction; "quiet" is specific to the reader's problem (racing mind, can't switch off). The double-verb issue with "I did Build the Quiet" was the main friction point.

**Community/identity names** (Sleep Builders, Quiet Builders, Clock Keepers, Night Shifters) — Sleep Builders emerged as the strongest because it's simple, unambiguous, and the community angle is unique among all candidates.

**No existing overt use found** — web search (June 2026) returned no consumer program, community, or trademark using "Sleep Builders."

## Book title context

Working book title: *Dead Tired Wide Awake — how to switch off your mind and sleep*

"Sleep Builders" works independently of this title and doesn't compete with it. The book title catches the exhausted reader; Sleep Builders is what they become once inside.

## Usage notes for content generation

- The program is always **Sleep Builders** (capitalised, no "the" required)
- Participants are **Sleep Builders** (identity, not just attendees)
- The verb is always **build** — avoid "fix," "hack," "optimise"
- The destination is **quiet** or **calm** — not "better sleep" or "good sleep"
- The mechanism is the **full 24-hour day** — not just bedtime habits
