# Ch06 thesis proposals: Melatonin

Ch06 is science with a personal frame. For the thesis it needs vocabulary fixes: 'well-regulated' (Decision 2), 'poor sleeper' used as a type of person (Decision 6), and 'fix' where it means the solution (Decision 3). One word at L37 connects the rhythm to training, using the same verb as Ch10 L15 ('Train the body clock'). The one-liner and fitness analogy stay out: this is a hormone chapter, and Ch04 has already made the point.
