# Part-structure rationalisation — proposal

**Type:** structural / signposting audit. **Status:** proposal only — `/book/` is read-only; apply by hand. Direction below is **decided** (four-part collapse); the five-part variant is retained only as a record of the alternative considered.

## What the book currently does

| Part heading | Lives in | Chapters it covers | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| *(no PART 1 heading anywhere)* | — | ch01 The Weight of It, ch02 The Turning Point | 2 |
| PART 2 – The Science | **bottom of ch02** (last line) | ch03–ch09 | 7 |
| PART 3 – The Program | top of ch10 | ch10 Taking Ownership | **1** |
| PART 4 – The habits | top of ch11 | ch11–ch25 | **15** |
| PART 5 – Over to you | top of ch26 | ch26 What Happens Next | 1 |

Inside PART 4 the real structure is carried by six **Phase** headings: Phase 1 *get set up* (ch11–13), Phase 2 *Remove Disruption* (ch14–16), Phase 3 *Train your body clock* (ch17–18), Phase 4 *train your nervous system* (ch19–20), Phase 5 *focus your energy* (ch21–22), Phase 6 *optimise your inputs* (ch23–25).

## The problems

1. **There is no Part 1.** The reader's first part marker is "PART 2".
2. **The Part 2 marker is misplaced.** It sits as a trailing line at the *bottom* of ch02, while every other Part heading sits at the *top* of a chapter — so its label appears to belong to the opening, not the science.
3. **Part 3 is a single chapter** (ch10, 935 words), then Part 4 begins one chapter later. A one-chapter Part reads as a mis-set heading.
4. **The Program / Habits split is a false division.** The habits *are* the program, not a sequel to it. Splitting "Part 3 – The Program" from "Part 4 – The habits" implies the habits come after the program rather than being its payload. "The habits" also opens with three chapters that aren't habits (Phase 1 set-up).

Net imbalance: **2 / 7 / 1 / 15 / 1**.

## Decision — collapse to four parts

The only hard break the reader needs in the back half is **science → action** (ch09 → ch10). The setup → habits transition (ch13 → ch14) is already marked by the Phase headings, so a second Part break there is redundant and is what creates the awkward Program-vs-Habits framing. Collapse the whole action half into one part and let the six Phases carry the internal rhythm.

| Part | Title | Chapters | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Problem** *(or: Where It Starts / The Wake-Up)* | ch01–ch02 | 2 |
| 2 | The Science | ch03–ch09 | 7 |
| 3 | The Program | ch10–ch25 | 16 |
| 4 | Over to You | ch26 | 1 |

ch10 (Taking Ownership) becomes the front door to the whole program; the Phases navigate from there. Part 3 is large, but in a do-the-work book the weight belongs in the doing, and six phase headings keep it from feeling monolithic — the imbalance is honest, not a flaw.

*Alternative considered and set aside:* keep five parts, renaming Part 3 → **Foundations** (ch10–13) and Part 4 → **The Habits** (ch14–25). This preserves a headline break at the ch14 "now it gets real" gear-change, but elevates a transition the Phases already handle. Not chosen.

## Cut the redundant three-jobs bridge in ch11

The paragraph at the top of ch11 —

> Everything that follows in Part 4 of this book is based on the three jobs from the last chapter: training the body clock, building the calming system, and stopping activation at the wrong times. The habits come one at a time.

— is to be **cut.** It duplicates ch10 line 15, which states the same framing more strongly and in the author's voice:

> So the habits do three jobs, the three signals from Chapter 4 turned into daily action. Train the body clock. Build the calming system. Stop activation at the wrong times. Every habit in the chapters ahead is one of those three.

The ch11 version's only non-duplicated function was acting as the Part 4 signpost; with the parts collapsed that job disappears. It is also slightly misplaced — it sits above **Phase 1 – get set up**, but getting set up is not one of the three jobs (those begin at Phase 2 / ch14). After the cut, ch10 ends on "turn to Chapter 11, and let's get to work" and ch11 opens straight into the tracker story; nothing is lost. (No need to relocate a trimmed version to ch14 — ch10 line 15 already carries it.)

## Edits required (for hand-application to /book/)

- **ch01** — add `# PART 1 – The Problem` above `# Chapter 1`.
- **ch02** — delete the trailing `# PART 2 – The Science` line.
- **ch03** — add `# PART 2 – The Science` above `# Chapter 3`, so all part markers sit consistently at chapter openings.
- **ch10** — no change to text; it now opens Part 3 (The Program). Confirm the existing `# PART 3 – The Program` heading stays.
- **ch11** — delete the `# PART 4 – The habits` heading **and** the redundant three-jobs paragraph beneath it; chapter now opens with `# Phase 1 – get set up` / `# Chapter 11`.
- **ch14, ch17, ch19, ch21, ch23** — Phase headings unchanged (they remain the internal navigation of Part 3). No renumbering needed under the four-part plan.
- **ch26** — change `# PART 5 – Over to you` to `# PART 4 – Over to You`.

Result: **2 / 7 / 16 / 1**, four parts, all markers at chapter tops, no orphan part, no Program/Habits false split, one duplicated paragraph removed.
