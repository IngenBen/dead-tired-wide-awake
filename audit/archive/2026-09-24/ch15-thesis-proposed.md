## 15-A · L33

**Why:** 'A good sleeper' names a type of person. The pair now matches: someone who sleeps well, someone already not sleeping well.  
**Decision ref:** Decision 6

*[L31]* There's a problem in the second part of the night too. After a heavier night of drinking, I would often crash out for the first three or four hours. Then wide awake. Three or four in the morning, heart going, no prospect of getting back to sleep.

▶ *[L33]* This is metabolic rebound: heart rate up, blood pressure up, the body shifting from suppressed to activated at the point in the night when it should be doing ~~it's~~ **its** recovery work. For ~~a good sleeper~~ **someone who sleeps well,** the rebound might mean a patch of light sleep before drifting off again. For someone already not sleeping well, it can mean that night's sleep is over.

*[L35]* If you do sleep through the metabolic rebound, you hit REM rebound.  The REM suppressed earlier springs back — often as more intense, vivid, sometimes disturbing dreams in the early hours. […]

**Clean:**

> This is metabolic rebound: heart rate up, blood pressure up, the body shifting from suppressed to activated at the point in the night when it should be doing its recovery work. For someone who sleeps well, the rebound might mean a patch of light sleep before drifting off again. For someone already not sleeping well, it can mean that night's sleep is over.
