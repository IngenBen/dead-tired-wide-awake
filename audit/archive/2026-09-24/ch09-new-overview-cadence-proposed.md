# PROPOSAL — Ch09 (new) "What Goes Wrong": "bad night" overview paragraph

> Status: proposed edit for author review. Not applied to `/book/`.
> Source chapter: `audit/ch09-new-what-goes-wrong-proposed.md`
> Finding: C&F 1 (cadence) + review note on vague "overnight hold".

## Location

"The bad night" section — the overview paragraph beginning "Cortisol won't fall…",
which sits between the "no magic fix" paragraph and the `![[graph - misaligned night]]`
placeholder.

## Issue

1. **Cadence.** All four sentences share one shape — statement, em-dash, appended
   consequence. Four in a row flatten into a monotone; the dash stops being emphasis
   and becomes the default beat.
2. **Vague term.** "the overnight hold runs low to begin with" leans on "hold" — Ch06's
   word for melatonin's mid-night plateau — before the idea has been introduced here,
   so in this position it reads as undefined jargon. The concept is properly set up
   later, in "Waking at 2am."

## Recommended change (vary the rhythm; cut the unintroduced "overnight hold")

Previous paragraph (unchanged, for context):

This is misalignment — and it can go very wrong. Once I understood this, I realised why there is no magic fix. How did I ever expect that right before bed, I could take one pill, or do one simple trick, and everything would fall back in to line for a solid eight hours of quality sleep?

Amended paragraph:

~~Cortisol won't fall — still activated, deep into the night. Dopamine keeps spiking with every check of the phone. Melatonin is rising, but fighting two Wake Executives that won't go home — and in long-term poor sleepers, the overnight hold runs low to begin with. Adenosine keeps building until the others finally take notice — too late to clear properly overnight.~~

**Cortisol won't fall. Still activated, deep into the night. Dopamine keeps spiking — every phone check another hit. Melatonin is rising, but it's outnumbered: two Wake Executives that won't go home. Adenosine just keeps building, until the others finally take notice. Too late to clear properly overnight.**

Following paragraph (unchanged, for context):

![[graph - misaligned night]]

Don't worry if that's a lot to take in at once. I'll break each one down below.

## Alternative — if you want to keep the melatonin detail here

Replace the cut clause with plain-English wording rather than dropping it. This also
echoes the chapter's own later line ("still plenty in the tank to carry through to morning"):

> Melatonin is rising, but it's outnumbered: two Wake Executives that won't go home, and in long-term poor sleepers **less of it to carry you through the night**.

## Notes

- The recommended version mixes a fragment, a dash and trailing fragments — no two
  sentences land the same way — and removes the undefined "overnight hold." It leads
  into the graph cleanly.
- Pairs with the Stage 1 recommendation to lighten this overview toward a teaser; if
  that fuller trim is taken, this paragraph may be reduced further still.
- Separate typo in the unchanged previous paragraph: "fall back in to line" → "into
  line" (logged under Weak points, not amended here).
