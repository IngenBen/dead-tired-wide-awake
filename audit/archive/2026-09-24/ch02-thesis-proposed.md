# Ch02 thesis proposals: The Turning Point

Ch02 is memoir. Its job for the thesis is to **set up** what Ch03 states: the search for 'the one thing', the realisation that the day matters, and the first appearance of *out of alignment*. It shouldn't state the fitness idea itself, because Ch03 L29 ('less like flipping a switch and more like getting fit') is the reveal, and it answers Ch02 L33 ('a magic switch'). The mountain biking (L23–25) is literal exercise, so the framing rule keeps the analogy away from it. The result is one word-level edit.

## Summary of changes

✅ **2-A APPROVED by the author 2026-09-24.**

- **2-A** (L41): 'That's where the fix had to be' uses 'fix' as the goal. It becomes 'the work'. L43 then carries the build verb ('I needed to build a good day'). Typo: 'thats'.

**No change, and why:**

- **L39** ('the whole system was out of alignment… their one thing was never going to work'): the first use of the diagnosis word, and it's already right under Decision 2. It also sets up Ch03 L35 ('as we saw in the last chapter, a single change can work, but only on a foundation that's already there'), so a fitness line here would duplicate Ch03 3-C ('in reasonable shape').
- **L47** ('holistic… incremental… one bad week didn't unravel the whole thing'): training principles in all but name, and 'holistic' and 'incremental' are deliberate repeated words (author-style-notes). Naming fitness here would pre-empt Ch03.
- **L67** ('one thing' against 'a program'): already makes the contrast. Ch03 does the fitness comparison.
- **L69** ('I will never be a good sleeper') and **L99** ('I'm just a bad sleeper'): both reject the identity label, which is what the thesis wants. They're not part of the identity-label question (that's about 'good/poor sleeper' used as shorthand).
- **L89** ('I still have bad weeks… habits slip… I know what to do about it'): already the resilience payoff, in plain words.
- **'Fix' (3 uses):** L17 'find a fix' and L95 'the failed fixes' describe the wrong approach (Decision 3, keep). L41 changes (2-A).
- **'Broken':** L91 'broken, poor-quality sleep' describes the sleep (Decision 3, keep).

## 2-A · L41

**Why:** 'Fix' is the goal here. 'The work' is plain and hands straight to L43's 'build'. L41 is one sentence, so the context runs two paragraphs each side.  
**Decision ref:** Decision 3

*[L37]* It reframed everything. Every tip I'd tried, every supplement, every bedtime routine. None of it was wrong exactly. Some of it was even well-supported by science.

*[L39]* Some people can try ‘one thing’ and swear by it. Most likely, they had a solid foundation. The tip worked because most of the system was already working.
