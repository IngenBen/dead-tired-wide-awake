## Summary

Four related changes arising from the three-pillars marketing frame ("train your body clock /
build your nervous system / remove disrupters") and a review of phase structure. The old Phase 5
("Focus your energy") is retired. Exercise moves to Phase 4; Social Media joins the renamed
Phase 5. Changes are in dependency order: the structural moves must be applied before the text
changes.

New phase structure:
- Phase 1 — Get set up (unchanged)
- Phase 2 — Remove disruption (unchanged)
- Phase 3 — Train your body clock (unchanged)
- Phase 4 — Train your nervous system (adds Exercise)
- Phase 5 — Optimise your inputs (formerly Phase 6; adds Social Media)

---

## Change 1 — Phase restructuring: Exercise to Phase 4, Social Media to Phase 5,
              old Phase 5 retired

**Rationale:** The old Phase 5 ("Focus your energy") grouped Exercise and Social Media under a
frame that fit neither cleanly. Exercise belongs in Phase 4 — the chapter makes the nervous
system argument explicitly (yoga uses the same vagus nerve channel as meditation, just in
motion; the timing rule is about managing activation load). Social Media belongs in Phase 5 as
a disrupter of both the nervous system (dopamine-cortisol activation) and the body clock
(melatonin suppression). "Optimise your inputs" is extended to cover mental inputs (content
consumed) alongside physical ones, which the chapter already supports through its existing
consumption language.

**What changes in the book:**

Remove the Phase 5 header from **ch21** (Exercise) — Phase 5 is retired:

~~# Phase 5 – focus your energy~~

Remove the Phase 6 header from **ch23** (Hydration) — Phase 6 is renumbered:

~~# Phase 6 – optimise your inputs~~

Add the Phase 5 header to **ch22** (Social Media) — this becomes the opening of Phase 5:

**# Phase 5 – optimise your inputs**

Phase 4 chapter order becomes: **ch19 Meditation → ch20 Napping → ch21 Exercise.**

Phase 5 chapter order becomes: **ch22 Social Media → ch23 Hydration → ch24 Food Timing →
ch25 Sugar.**

## Change 2 — New Phase 5 intro (to sit between the Phase 5 header and ch22 body text)

**Rationale:** Phase 5 needs a brief opener that frames it as a return to disruption removal —
the subtler layer, sequenced last because the earlier phases needed to work first. This closes
the structural loop with Phase 2 and explains the sequencing logic rather than leaving the
reader to infer it.

**Add the following paragraphs between the Phase 5 header and the first line of ch22:**

---

Phase 2 cleared the biggest disruptors. Phase 5 clears the rest.

These habits are subtler — each generating its own quiet cortisol signal, interfering with the
body's ability to cool and settle, or feeding the Wake Executives in ways that compound through
the night. They come last not because they matter less, but because the earlier phases needed
to come first — to give you a little more progress on your sleep, restore a little more
willpower, and make the removal actually register.

---

## Change 3 — Ch22 (Social Media) opening: update phase reference and add framing sentence

**Rationale:** The chapter's existing content supports the "mental inputs" reading (consumption
language is already present; the three steps are essentially don't consume at night / change
what you consume / consume less). But the opening currently leads with blue light and melatonin,
which pulls toward a disruption frame before the reader has the inputs lens. One sentence
positions social media explicitly as a mental input, parallel to the physical inputs around it.

**Location:** Line 1 of ch22, then a new sentence added after the first paragraph.

Edit line 1 of ch22 to reflect the new phase:

~~Social media is the last habit in Phase 5~~ **Social media is the first habit in Phase 5**
— and probably the one you've heard the most about already.

Add a new sentence after that paragraph:

**Food, hydration, sugar — this phase is about what you put into your body. Social media is
about what you put into your mind.**
