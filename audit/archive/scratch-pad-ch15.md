## STAGE 1 — Beat analysis

### Main messages of the chapter

1. When you can't sleep, don't lie in bed struggling — get up.
2. Go to a dim space and do something only just engaging enough to distract you, not enough to activate you.
3. Why lying there fails: frustration raises cortisol, and time spent awake in bed erodes the bed-equals-sleep association.
4. The two mistakes the author made — staying in bed (the cortisol/thinking loop), then getting up but bingeing engaging TV (dopamine, and cortisol too).
5. The routine works on two timescales: it breaks tonight's spiral and rewrites the association over weeks.
6. It matches established therapy (CBT-i / ACT-i).

Beats not listed below are pulling their weight and are left alone per the prompt.

**Cadence 2 — L35, one block of ~8 sentences.** Style notes: rarely more than three or four sentences per block. After the therapy consolidation this paragraph is dense. Two paragraph breaks fix it (2 / 4 / 4 sentences):

CBT-i — cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia — makes "don't lie in bed awake; get up" a central recommendation.
