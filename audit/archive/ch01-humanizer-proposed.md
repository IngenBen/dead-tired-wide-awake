# Chapter 1 humanizer pass: proposed edits

Source: `/book/ch01 - The Weight of It.md` (version last modified 2026-08-14). Proposals only; apply by hand.
Skipped by author: original items 6 and 7 (the "Zen-like intuition" contrast and the repeated "It was more like").
Items keep their original numbers so they match the chat review.

## 1. Spaced hyphen used as a dash

Paragraph: "Despite being a glass-half-full kind of person..."

Before: Getting irritated by things that really shouldn't have bothered me - I was good at filtering what came out… most of the time.

After: Getting irritated by things that shouldn't have bothered me. I was good at filtering what came out… most of the time.

## 2. Spaced hyphen, end of a list

Paragraph: "All the masking, the pushing through..."

Before: All the masking, the pushing through, the filtering, the disconnection - and all the while life never stopped.

After: All the masking, the pushing through, the filtering, the disconnection, and all the while life never stopped.

## 3. Em dash

Paragraph: "The weight was killing me."

Before: Figuratively, in the day to day — but literally too.

After: Figuratively, in the day to day, but literally too.

## 4. Em dash before a short reversal

Paragraph: "You would think falling asleep..."

Before: You would think falling asleep the moment my head hit the pillow would be a good thing — it wasn't. It meant I was running such a huge sleep deficit my body would just crash.

After: You would think falling asleep the moment my head hit the pillow would be a good thing. It wasn't. It meant I was running such a huge sleep deficit my body would just crash.

## 5. En dash

Paragraph: "The days were their own kind of punishment."

Before: I tried to nap whenever I could – weekends and commuting to work, mostly.

After: I tried to nap whenever I could, mostly on weekends and while commuting to work.

## 8. Closer that restates the scene before it

Paragraph: final two paragraphs before "I didn't know if things could ever change."

Before: That was it. That was the whole problem, the way it took over every important moment of my life.

After: That was the whole problem: it took over every important moment of my life.

## Left alone on purpose

- Fragment runs ("Tired but wired...", "A vitamin deficiency. A parasite...", the four dream fragments): each carries different content and matches the style notes.
- "deeply exhausting" and "completely overwhelming": near-fillers, but they sound like the author.
- Final line "I didn't know if things could ever change.": plain and specific.
- Reading-to-the-kids paragraph: no change; kept as is.

## Not a humanizer issue (not proposed)

"...when I woke, my rational mind took over and settled me, I knew none of it had been real" is a comma splice. Not included unless requested.
