# Claims List — Referenceability Ranking (scratch pad)

Working list for the blind-endnotes project. Claims from `/book/` (ch01–ch25), in order
of appearance. **LOW items have been removed** — this list now holds only the claims that
warrant a reference or further reading.

Numbers preserve each claim's original position in its chapter (so there are gaps where
LOW items were deleted, and Ch25 drops out entirely — its claims were all LOW).

**Ranking key**
- **[HIGH]** — a strong, specific claim to fact (statistic, named finding, mechanism,
  dosage, clinical fact). A skeptical reader would expect a citation. Must-source endnotes.
- **[MED]** — a general scientific/mechanistic claim (broadly accepted but worth a pointer
  for further reading), a contested claim, or a counterintuitive/uncommon assertion a
  reader might not expect.
