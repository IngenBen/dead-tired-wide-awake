(`claim-references.md`), transcribed from that file. Grouped: **A.** authoritative bodies
(T1/T2); **B.** primary literature & reviews (T3–T5); **C.** claims with no qualifying source.

Format: **claim(s) supported** — Source — link. DOI resolvers (`https://doi.org/…`) are given
where the HIGH file recorded a DOI; PubMed (PMID) links where it recorded a PMID.
