Where a clean DOI/URL was **not** pinned this session, it's flagged inline ("source to firm up"): **X3** (common-culprit food list) and **D4** (light-therapy lamp) — both should get a clean authoritative citation before publication.

- DOIs are given as `https://doi.org/...` resolvers where available; these are the most stable form.
