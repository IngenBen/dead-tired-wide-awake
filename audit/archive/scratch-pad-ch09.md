## STAGE 1 — Beat analysis (problem beats only)

**Beat: "the habits do three jobs" paragraph (line 14) — [criticality 3/5]**
This is the load-bearing reframing beat and it is doing too much at once: it introduces the two-settings handover, defines three jobs, AND inserts a technical caveat ("Strictly, your body clock is a separate system from your nervous system — but it's close enough to picture three jobs"). The caveat is exactly the kind of hedge/over-explanation the author normally cuts (author-style-notes: "when in doubt, cut the elaboration"; "trust the reader"). It momentarily undercuts the framing it is trying to establish.
Fix:
> So the habits do three jobs. Some build the calming system. Some stop you propping up the activating system when you shouldn't. And some keep your clock accurate, so the swap lands on time. ~~Strictly, your body clock is a separate system from your nervous system — but it's close enough to picture three jobs: wind down, stop winding up, keep time.~~ **Three jobs: wind down, stop winding up, keep time.** Every habit ahead is one of those three. Once you can see which, you'll know why it's there — and you'll be able to weigh up any new tip someone throws at you.

**Beat: removal of the concrete "pill / routine / youtube expert" list — [criticality 2/5]**
Book/ch09 grounded the "looking outside myself" idea with a stacked-fragment personal list ("A pill. A routine I'd read about somewhere. A youtube expert who'd tell me the one thing I was missing."). The proposed flattens this to "None of what I'd tried had stuck." That stacked-fragment specificity is a signature device (author-style-notes: stacked fragments, concrete specifics). The chapter still meets the personal-grounding minimum via the massage example, so this is a voice/texture loss, not a structural failure — but it is a real downgrade.
Fix:
> That felt heavy but understanding it was actually a relief. **I'd spent years looking for something outside myself to fix it. A pill. A routine I'd read about somewhere. A youtube expert with the one thing I was missing.** None of ~~what I'd tried had stuck~~ **it stuck**, because none of it had addressed the whole company.

## STAGE 2 — Category review

### Contradictions

**Contradiction 1 — habit count: "Twelve habits" vs "sixteen" everywhere else — [criticality 5/5]**
Proposed line 16: "Twelve habits." The rest of the manuscript still says sixteen: ch24 ("Twenty-four chapters, sixteen habits, six phases. The program is built."), ch25 ("across all six phases"), index.md / wiki the-program.md ("16-habit, 6-phase sequenced program"). If 12 is the intended new number, ch24, ch25, index.md and the-program.md must all be updated in lockstep or the book contradicts itself at the payoff. This is the headline finding.

**Contradiction 2 — the six phases are no longer named/enumerated, but the rest of the book still relies on six phases — [criticality 4/5]**
Book/ch09 listed all six phases with one-line descriptions (Get set up → Clear disrupters → Build rhythm → Train nervous system → Direct energy → What goes in). The proposed deletes the entire enumeration, jumps from general framing straight to "# Phase 1 – get set up", and the Part 4 header (line 48) drops "six phases" too. Yet ch24 and ch25 explicitly reference "six phases", and the phase headers still exist in the manuscript. As written, the reader is given phase headings with no roadmap of how many phases there are or what they are. Either the phase scaffold is being removed (then ch24/ch25/phase headers must change) or the enumeration needs to stay. Right now it is half-removed.

**Contradiction 3 — the "three jobs" naming diverges from the canonical three categories — [criticality 3/5]**
Proposed reframes the three categories as "wind down, stop winding up, keep time" (build calming system / stop propping activating system / keep clock accurate). Book/ch09, ch25, and wiki/three-program-categories.md name them "body clock / nervous system / remove disrupters." The mapping is close but not clean — "stop winding up" is not the same set as "remove disrupters", and ch25's closing reframes them yet again ("removing what was getting in the way, training your body clock, training your nervous system"). Three different namings of the same trichotomy now exist across ch09-proposed, ch25, and the wiki. Pick one canonical wording and propagate.

**Contradiction 4 — "four executives" dropped from this chapter's framing — [criticality 2/5]**
Book/ch09 hinged on "get the four executives working together." Proposed reduces this to "the company and its executives" (line 8) and the Part 4 header drops the executives entirely. The four executives remain load-bearing in ch04 and ch08. Not necessarily wrong (this chapter pivots to the nervous-system framing), but flag that the executive framing and the new two-settings framing now sit side by side without being reconciled for the reader.
