### Contradictions

**Contradiction 1 — Terminology mismatch with ch21: "curious-good" vs "hyped-good"**

Ch08 introduces the distinction between two types of positive dopamine content: outrage/shock content (described as "fast, volatile spikes — the neurological equivalent of a shove") and curiosity/educational content (described as "slower, warmer ones — more like a sustained hum"). The chapter labels only the second state: "curious-good." It labels the desired resting state: "calm-good." The first state (outrage content) is never given a name in ch08.

Ch21 then references this as follows: *"This is the calm-good versus hyped-good distinction from Chapter 8."* It also uses: *"I was seeking calm-good, not worked up."*

The problem: "hyped-good" does not appear anywhere in ch08. Ch21 attributes a term to ch08 that ch08 does not contain. A reader who follows the cross-reference will not find the term they were sent back to find.

The fix must happen in ch08, since that is the coinage chapter. Either:
- Add "hyped-good" as the label for outrage/shock content in ch08, making the three-state model explicit (hyped-good / curious-good / calm-good); or
- Simplify to two states in ch08 (hyped-good / calm-good, folding curious content into hyped) and adjust the curious-good language to match.

Option 2 is simpler and aligns with ch21's two-state framing. The curious-good distinction (educational vs outrage) remains in the prose — it's the story — but the named labels are hyped vs calm.

*Proposed fix (in the curious-good passage):*

Before: *"Both types of content were doing the same thing to the same system."*

> ~~But feeling curious-good is not the same as feeling calm-good. One is a system winding down. The other has found something worth pursuing — *this is interesting, I want to know more* — and has no intention of stopping.~~

> **But curious-good and outrage-good are both hyped-good. Neither is calm-good. Calm-good is a system winding down — nothing to pursue, nowhere to go. Curious-good has found something worth chasing and has no intention of stopping. Different flavour. Same direction.**

After: *"This is fine during the day, but not when you should be getting off to sleep."*

This introduces "hyped-good" as an umbrella term that ch21 can then reference correctly.
