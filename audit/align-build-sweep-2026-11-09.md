
## Part 4: "signals" vs training (added 2026-11-11)

70 uses in the book (not counting endnotes), heaviest in Ch04, Ch06, Ch18 and Ch20. Most are physiological: melatonin as a signal, the bladder signal, and so on. The framework uses are Ch04 L7, L9, L67, L75, L77 · Ch10 L15 · Ch18 L13/17/39/43 · Ch19 L3/9/25 · Ch20 L105.

**Fit with fitness: yes, if the relationship is stated.** A signal is a single input, like one rep. Training is the same signals sent consistently until the system adapts. The book already behaves this way (Ch18 L39 "inconsistent signal"; Ch19 L25 and Ch20 L105 repetition turning into a cue), but it never says it outright.

**Where a reader could stumble**
1. **Ch04 L9** names the program as signals ("Learning when and how to send each one is what the program in this book is all about"). Ch26 L35 names it a "training program". Nothing joins the two.
2. **Ch10 L15** is the handover ("the three signals from Chapter 4 turned into daily action") but the jobs don't map 1:1 onto the signals: *activate* → "Stop activation at the wrong times"; *timing* → "Train the body clock"; *calm* → "Build the calming system". Activation is the one that turns around: in Ch04 it's a signal you send, in Ch10 it's something you stop. That is only reconciled later, in Ch18 L45 (activating "on purpose, early").
3. **Ch20 L25** rejects calming by "signalling to your body that it's not under threat" in the moment. A reader holding Ch04's "a signal to calm" could hear that as a contradiction. Ch20 L87 ("The attention itself is the signal") partly rescues it.

**Proposed (one bridge, two light touches)**
- Ch04 L9: after "...what the program in this book is all about." add a line making signals → training explicit, e.g. *"Send them once and not much changes. Send them every day and the company learns. That's training."*
- Ch10 L15: make the mapping visible, e.g. *"...the three signals from Chapter 4, sent day after day: timing trains the body clock, calm builds the calming system, and activation gets moved to the right times."*
- Ch20 L25: optional, e.g. "signalling in the moment" → no change needed if L87 stays.
Ch04 L65–67 (once the one-liner is restored) already sets it up: "nothing in my day had ever sent the right signals" → "Not broken, just not well trained."

### Part 4b: option to retire "signals" as a framework term (2026-11-11)
Assessment: as a *named framework* ("three signals"), signals add a layer without adding a lever. The CEO → three signals → three jobs → 12 habits chain can become CEO → three jobs → 12 habits. Keep "signal" as plain physiology and everyday English (melatonin is a signal, morning light signal, bladder signal, "the wrong signal"). Those don't ask the reader to hold a concept.
Idea worth keeping from Ch04 L7: you can't operate the machinery directly; you shape the day and the company produces sleep on its own (indirect control).
Lines to reword: Ch04 L7, L9, L67, L75, L77 · Ch10 L15. Ch04 L9 and L75 would introduce the three jobs (train the body clock, build the calming system, keep activation to the right times) instead of three signals.
Downstream if adopted: wiki pages four-executives, sleep-incorporated-metaphor, calming-activating-systems, and index.md mention "three signals".

### Part 4c: outcome (2026-11-11)
Signals retired as a framework term. Replaced with culture ("everyone works together") and three jobs stated as retraining. Marked up as tracked changes in book/ ch04, ch10, ch26; see log.md.
