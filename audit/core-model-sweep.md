## Patterns

- **Label drift is the only recurring problem.** In descriptive prose the activating/calming pair slides to "side" (Ch06 L29, Ch18 L45), "the calm" / "the activation" (Ch20 L3), "calm takes over" (Ch18 L45) and "the activated state" (Ch20 L51, endnote L885). Plain adjectives are fine ("still activated", "you're activated", "go-mode", "super activated": Ch07 L41, Ch08 L39, Ch09 L15/L28, Ch23 L31). They only become a problem when they stand in for the system names.
- **"Align" survivors.** Two uses as the concept (Ch02 L41, Ch16 L31), one literal use (Ch06 L25) and one filename (Ch04 L47). After these, the term is gone from the chapters.
- **Company vocabulary is strong and consistent.** It always runs "working together / against each other", and the staff are never useless (Ch22 L7 "working flat out", Ch25 L29 "overworked"). Wake-side-only wording ("go home", "left the building", "clocked off") appears only in cortisol or dopamine passages, which the rules allow.
- **The third job is only voiced on the wake side.** The core model says the disruptors call the wrong executives in on both sides. In the book, wake-side disruptors get company wording ("call the Wake Executives back in": Ch04 L77, Ch17 L12/L40/L48, Ch19 L19). Sleep-side disruptors are framed only as signals or adenosine (sleep-ins Ch18 L39 "an inconsistent signal to the executives"; long naps Ch21 L15 "the Sleep Executives don't have enough to work with"). Nothing breaks a rule. But if you want the both-sides version visible, Ch21 L15 is the natural place for a line about calling the sleep side in at the wrong time.
- **"Signal" carries two meanings in Ch05–Ch08.** Nearly half the Signals hits are biological (melatonin "is a signal", Ch06 L17). They are listed as Literal, and they don't clash with the model because Ch06 L49 bridges them ("give the system the signals it needs"). This is worth knowing for the wiki rewrite.
- **Training and fitness are clean.** There's no "reps", no "out of practice" and no "sleep fit". "Practice" only appears as "In practice". "Repetition" appears only in the conditioning passages (Ch07 L57, Ch17 L28/L60). Fitness stays inside Ch03, Ch20, the Ch21/Ch22 echoes and Ch26 L9. The executives never do push-ups.
